Biography

I am Assistant Professor in the Department of Informatics at Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María in Santiago, Chile. I also serve as an Adjunct Senior Lecturer in the Department of Medical Imaging and Radiation Sciences at Monash University, Australia, and as a Principal Investigator at the Millennium Institute for Intelligent Healthcare Engineering (iHEALTH).

I possess a multidisciplinary academic background that seamlessly integrates engineering with data science. I began my career at the Universidad de Valparaíso, where I earned my degree in Civil Biomedical Engineering in 2009 and a Master of Engineering Sciences specializing in Biomedical Engineering in 2011.

In 2016, I obtained my PhD and a second Master of Engineering Sciences from the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile (PUC). Following my doctoral studies, I completed a four-year postdoctoral fellowship (2016–2020) at PUC’s Biomedical Imaging Center (CIB) and the Department of Electrical Engineering. During this tenure, I also served as a researcher at the Millennium Nucleus CardioMR, focusing on the intersection of medical technology and clinical research. To further expand my technical expertise, I earned a Master of Data Science from the Universidad del Desarrollo in 2022.

My primary research interests include biomedical image processing, cardiovascular magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), and biomechanics. Over the past 12 years, I have developed several numerical methods for extracting quantitative information from 4D Flow MRI data, with a dedicated focus on accelerating the clinical translation of these developments. I have participated in numerous high-impact projects related to cardiovascular MRI, including 4D Flow MRI, cardiac strain quantification, and cardiovascular mechanics.

Conferences & Presentations

Throughout his career, Dr. Sotelo has been an active contributor to the scientific community, presenting his research at numerous prestigious national and international conferences. His work has been featured in over 80 presentations and workshops, including multiple editions of the ISMRM Annual Meeting & Exhibition across the globe (e.g., Singapore, Honolulu, Paris, Montreal, London, and Toronto), the SCMR Annual Scientific Sessions, and EuroCMR. His presentations cover a wide array of topics, from advanced 4D Flow MRI quantification methods, finite element methods, and AI applied to medical imaging, to hands-on workshops and clinical symposiums. Additionally, he frequently participates in national events such as the Chilean Congress of Radiology, the Chilean Congress of Cardiology and Cardiovascular Surgery, and various interdisciplinary engineering and biomechanics symposiums, highlighting his commitment to advancing medical imaging technologies and clinical practices both in Chile and internationally.
